## Release-18 measurement of #269 on top of the shipped collector

We selected ordinary full-workspace `cargo build` after warming unmodified uv `c22e6b71`. Both Cargo drivers used Ohm’s distributed release-18 compiler `a4fa11bc` and the same release-style flags. The predecessor applies #264, #267 and #268 to `7caf3991`. The candidate adds precisely #269. Ohm’s shipping PGO profile was unavailable. The comparisons use one stable Cargo executable path per cohort and independent source, registry and target roots for the two counters.

The incremental reduction passed the prespecified gate in both setups. The intervals have at least 95% coverage. Each native exec trace showed Cargo as the only program, and every counted command remained fresh. Native `strace` recorded successful reads of `target/debug/uv.d` in both arms of each cohort. Forcing its removal and regeneration preserved all 74 public depfiles byte for byte on the initial root and all 74 on the replica. The regenerated `uv.d` files were 35,103 and 54,217 bytes, respectively, because their absolute source and dependency paths differ. The numbers describe the Cargo process and its threads on a warm default build.
